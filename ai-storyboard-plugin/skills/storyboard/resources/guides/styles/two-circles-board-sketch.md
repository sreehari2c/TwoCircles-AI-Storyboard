# Two Circles board sketch

Status: Required default style

Use the supplied storyboard assets as the visual contract:

- Layout reference: `resources/templates/Storyboard_Template.jpg`
- Art-style reference: `resources/templates/Storyboard Example.png`

## Board

Black presentation-board background with strong white typography. Keep the storyboard/title area and project/title area readable and prominent. Use the fixed 12-frame board as the default single-image layout.

## Frame count

For 1-12 planned frames, fill only the needed panels and leave all unused panel slots as black background. Do not invent extra filler beats just to fill the board.

For more than 12 planned frames, either split the sequence into multiple storyboard images or resize/reflow panels deliberately. Record the chosen layout in the plan, prompt packet, result metadata, and export notes.

## Panels

Grayscale pencil-and-ink storyboard sketches on subtle paper texture. Use confident linework, cross-hatching, strong value contrast, readable silhouettes, and clear cinematic blocking. Panels may include close-ups, inserts, wide establishing frames, and match cuts when the story calls for them.

## Captions

Place short white captions under each filled panel. Keep captions direct, production-readable, and short enough to fit comfortably below the panel.

## Prohibited traits

Avoid full-color rendering, photorealism, painterly concept art, cluttered decorative detail, low-contrast sketches, illegible captions, filler frames, and non-black unused template slots.
