# Prompt Packet — v001

Project: Luxury Golf Resort — Sunrise Pitch Frames  
Aspect ratio: 16:9 broadcast  
Use case: ads-marketing / storyboard pitch contact sheet  
Style: photorealistic premium commercial realism  

## Continuity locks

- Warm sunrise from camera-left/back-left.
- Deep emerald fairway, champagne-gold highlights, clear calm morning.
- Exactly one golfer and exactly one caddie.
- Golfer in cream/neutral styling; caddie in navy styling.
- Walking direction remains left-to-right.
- No logos, signage, crowds, extra golfers, or invented brand claims.

## Generated contact sheet prompt

Create one coherent six-panel cinematic contact sheet arranged as a clean 3x2 grid of distinct widescreen panels. Panel 1 is an extreme-wide empty fairway with dew and resort architecture; panel 2 is a wide lateral view of one golfer and one caddie walking left-to-right; panel 3 is macro dew detail with the pair blurred behind; panel 4 is an intimate glove-preparation detail; panel 5 is a medium-wide tracking view into sunrise; panel 6 is a tight club handoff with clean end-card negative space. Maintain consistent sunrise, wardrobe, subjects, fairway, premium commercial realism, natural anatomy, restrained lens flare, and no text or logos.
