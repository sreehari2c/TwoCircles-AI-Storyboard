# Luxury Golf Resort — Sunrise Pitch Frames

## Project Summary

Six 16:9 broadcast pitch frames for a premium golf resort advertisement. The sequence begins with controlled landscape scale, introduces a solitary player and caddie, then narrows into dew, ritual, and quiet human detail before resolving on the pair moving into the course.

## Creative Constants

- Photorealistic live-action premium commercial realism.
- Warm sunrise key from camera-left/back-left; readable shadows, restrained bloom.
- Deep emerald fairway, champagne-gold light, neutral cream/navy wardrobe.
- One golfer and one caddie only; empty course, no invented signage or claims.
- Screen direction: player and caddie travel left-to-right in walking frames.
- 16:9 broadcast-safe composition with clean negative space.

## Production Assumptions

- Six frames inferred from the general concept.
- 23.976 fps inferred for broadcast pitch language; no slow motion required.
- Location, wardrobe, exact resort architecture, and brand identity remain generic/inferred.
- Visual generation is photorealistic pitch art, not a final location scout or locked production design.

## Story Progression

1. Establish the resort and the untouched morning.
2. Introduce the player and caddie as a small human presence in the landscape.
3. Move into tactile dew and turf detail.
4. Show the player’s quiet preparation with the caddie nearby.
5. Follow their composed walk down the fairway.
6. Resolve on an intimate, aspirational gesture as the course opens ahead.
